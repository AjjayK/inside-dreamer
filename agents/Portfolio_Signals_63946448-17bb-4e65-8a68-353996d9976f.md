# Agent: Portfolio Signals

**ID:** 63946448-17bb-4e65-8a68-353996d9976f

## Description

# OVERVIEW
A weekly portfolio intelligence agent that monitors direct sources—SEC filings, earnings data, regulatory updates, and central bank decisions—to surface signals most likely to impact your holdings. Organizes insights hierarchically by sector, then company, so you understand macro context before drilling down into individual positions.

# KEY FEATURES
- **Direct Source Data**, Pulls from SEC filings, earnings reports, regulatory bodies, and central bank communications—no news aggregators or blogs, only primary sources
- **Sector-First Organization**, Groups insights by industry and macro trends first, then by individual company holdings, giving you context before company-specific details
- **Multi-Signal Monitoring**, Tracks company news (earnings, press releases, SEC filings), peer/sector updates, and macro drivers (government actions, regulation, fiscal policy, central bank decisions)
- **Weekly Briefing**, Automated weekly summary delivered to your feed, highlighting only the signals most likely to move your portfolio

# VERBATIM INSTRUCTIONS
I want a finance agent that links to my holdings and produces a weekly summary of the signals most likely to impact my portfolio: company news (earnings, press releases, SEC filings), sector/peer updates, and macro trends—especially those tied to government actions, regulation, fiscal policy, and central-bank decisions. Most importantly the agent needs to use the direct sources of data and not any news articles or blogs like how present agents do.

Rather than analyzing everything strictly company-by-company, the output should be hierarchically organized—first by Sector/Industry, then by Company—so I can understand the broader context before drilling down into individual holdings.
